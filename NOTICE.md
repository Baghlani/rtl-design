## Prior art & structural inspiration

No content (rules, values, or prose) was copied from any other project. All design
knowledge in this skill derives from the author's own Persian/RTL product work.
The *form factor* — how a good agent skill is shaped — openly builds on the work of:

- **Agent Skills specification** (agentskills.io, Apache-2.0 / CC-BY-4.0) — the standard
  itself: SKILL.md anatomy, progressive disclosure, scripts/references conventions.
- **impeccable** by Paul Bakaus (Apache-2.0) — the lean dispatcher body + per-topic
  references + deterministic no-LLM detector pattern, and the precedent of crediting
  one's origins in a NOTICE file.
- **ui-ux-pro-max** by nextlevelbuilder (MIT) — the zero-token knowledge-base pattern
  (bulk knowledge external to the loaded body) and the font-pairing recipe format.
- **taste-skill** by Leonxlnx (MIT) — the anti-slop framing and user-tunable intensity
  dials.
- **frontend-design** by Anthropic (anthropics/skills, Apache-2.0) — the original
  design-skill prose style and restraint principles.
